Every few months another list of "the 12 best AI note-takers" appears, and almost all of them are the same twelve tools ranked in whatever order pays the writer best. That's not useful when you have a call in ten minutes and just want notes you don't have to take yourself.

So we did the boring version. We took the eight tools people actually shortlist, and judged them on the three things that decide this in practice: **is the transcription good enough to trust, how far does the free plan really get you, and does a bot barge into your meeting?** Everything else — integrations, dashboards, "AI insights" — matters only after those three.

## The short version

For most people, **Fathom** is the one to start with. Its free plan records without a meeting cap, the summaries are clean enough to paste straight into a follow-up email, and you only pay if you outgrow it. That combination — low commitment, real usefulness on day one — is exactly what most people want and rarely what these lists recommend, because the free tool pays the least.

It is not the right answer for everyone, and the honest part is saying when it isn't.

## When it shouldn't be Fathom

**If your notes need to live in your CRM,** or your calls happen in many languages, **Fireflies** is the better tool. It has the deepest integrations, handles 100+ languages, and is built for teams who work inside the transcript rather than glancing at a summary. It also, for what it's worth, runs the most generous affiliate program of any tool here — which is precisely why we're careful to say it's the runner-up, not the pick. It wins for teams, not for most individuals.

**If a bot appearing in your meeting is a problem** — sensitive conversations, client calls, anything where "Fathom Notetaker has joined" is a bad look — **Granola** is the one. It doesn't join the call at all; it listens through your device and shapes the notes around what you actually typed during the meeting. The catch is that it's Mac-first and its free tier is tighter.

## The honest caveat

No AI note-taker is perfect on messy audio, heavy accents or crosstalk, and none removes your responsibility to skim before you forward. Treat every summary as a very good first draft. Used that way, the right one of these genuinely gives you back the ten minutes after every call you used to spend writing up what just happened.
